# THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Wednesday, 25 November 2026

A broadsheet newspaper summing up 25 November 2026 in one image: 30 verified
stories across 4 regions, drawn entirely in flat vector with PIL.

- **3 heroes:** Cartagena supermarket siege, CIA + ODNI cyberattack, Iowa
  lead-azide detonation (9,500 lbs, biggest blast in ~50 years).
- **6 more heroes:** NATO space defense, Russia accuses Ukraine of "terrorism",
  South Korea minister resigns, Australia–Solomon Islands pact, Vaibhav's
  204 off 53 balls, Dutch court keeps climate targets binding.
- **Map:** every story plotted at its true lon/lat — clear continents with
  coastlines + islands, numbered dots with leader lines where they collide.
- **Also on Wednesday:** stories 10–30 in two columns.
- **The Day in Numbers:** 30 hostages, 9,500 lbs, 74,746, 266M, 204 runs, 10 of 12.
- **Date Check:** stories rejected for the wrong date, listed honestly.

`build.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG). `main.py` writes it to
`/output/entry.png`. Every story happened or was first reported on
25 November 2026; research cutoff ~06:15 UTC.
